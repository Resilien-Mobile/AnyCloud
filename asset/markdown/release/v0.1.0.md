# AnyCloud v0.1.0 发布：待办、电量与坚持，常驻通知栏

Welcome to AnyCloud v0.1.0!

AnyCloud v0.1.0 is the first release of a notification-first companion. It turns quick thoughts into ongoing reminders, keeps task progress visible through Live Updates, and shows battery and charging details at a glance.

欢迎来到 AnyCloud v0.1.0！

AnyCloud v0.1.0 是首个版本，围绕通知栏打造一个随身效率工具：把临时想法变成常驻提醒，让任务进度通过 Live Update 实时可见，并将电量与充电状态汇总到一屏之内。

---

### What's New in v0.1.0

- **Any Cloud: Keep Quick Reminders Where You Can See Them**:
  - Type a thought into the bottom bar and turn it into an ongoing notification.
  - Finish an item with a checkbox, or swipe to remove it when it is no longer needed.
  - The navigation drawer keeps running totals and an annual effort track built from your daily activity.

- **Task Cloud: Task Progress, Always Up to Date**:
  - Tasks are delivered as Live Update notifications with completed and remaining counts.
  - Reorder items with a long press and drag.
  - Creating, completing, or deleting a task immediately refreshes the notification.

- **Power Cloud: Battery and Charging at a Glance**:
  - See adapter type, real-time wattage, and battery percentage in one compact row.
  - Enable Power Cloud to keep a continuously updated power notification.
  - The current watt value refreshes automatically while the service is active.

### v0.1.0 更新内容

- **Any Cloud：让随手记下的事情常驻可见**:
  - 在底部输入框写下内容，即可生成常驻通知。
  - 用勾选框标记完成，不需要时左滑即可删除。
  - 抽屉中持续统计累计天数和累计规划次数，并通过年度努力轨迹展示坚持情况。

- **Task Cloud：任务进度始终保持最新**:
  - 任务通过 Live Update 通知展示，包含已完成与剩余数量。
  - 长按拖动即可调整任务顺序。
  - 新增、完成或删除任务后，通知会立即刷新。

- **Power Cloud：电量与充电状态一屏掌握**:
  - 一眼查看适配器类型、实时功率和电池百分比。
  - 开启 Power Cloud 后，功率通知会持续更新。
  - 当前瓦数会在服务运行期间自动刷新。

### Known Limitations

v0.1.0 is still in a very early development stage. Some buttons and animations are not fully operational yet.

Subsequent v0.1.x updates will address these issues.

### 暂时不足

0.1.0 还处在很早期的开发状态，某些按钮和动画暂时无法操作。

后续大量 0.1.x 更新将会修复这些问题。
